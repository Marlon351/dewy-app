Dewy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Dewy helps you build and keep a skincare routine. This policy explains what information Dewy collects, how it's used and protected, and the choices you have. Dewy is operated by Marlon Rodriguez ("we", "us").

Information we collect

  • Account information: your first name, last name, email address and password. Your password is stored only as a secure one-way hash, so nobody (including us) can read it.
  • Your routine: the steps in your morning and evening routines, the products you add, and the days each step is scheduled.
  • Your progress: which steps you complete each day, your daily skin rating, and any notes you write.
  • Technical information: your IP address is used briefly to protect the service from abuse (for example, limiting repeated login attempts). We do not store it with your account.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising tools.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and log you in.
  • To save your routine and progress and sync them to any device you log in on.
  • To send you emails you ask for, such as password reset codes.

We do not sell your information, share it for advertising, or use it to build profiles about you.

How we protect your information

  • All data is sent between the app and our servers over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Your routine step names, product names, skin ratings and notes are encrypted (AES-256) before they are stored, because they can reveal personal health information.
  • Your data can only be accessed with your own login, and your login session is stored in your device's secure storage (Keychain / Keystore).

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you if a breach affects your data as required by law.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run Dewy. They process data only on our behalf:

  • MongoDB Atlas stores the app's database.
  • Render hosts the app's server.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers password reset emails.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Account → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, routine and all progress data from our database.

Your rights

You can view and update your name, email and password in the app under Account → Edit Profile. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, export or delete your data. To make a request, contact us at the email below.

Children

Dewy is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes to this policy, we will update the date above and let you know in the app.
